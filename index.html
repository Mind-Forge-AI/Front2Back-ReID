01 / THE CHALLENGE

Recognition beyond a shared view.

A vehicle leaves one camera and reappears in another with no shared field of view. Front2Back-ReID asks a model to connect those views under realistic changes in viewpoint, scale, occlusion and traffic.

03 / EVALUATION

Three ways to see the query.

Every pair provides the original marked view, a target crop and a segmentation silhouette. The frozen test set supports top-1 candidate selection across 500 recorded galleries.

RGBFull front image with target overlay.
CROPPadded crop around the target vehicle.
MASKTarget silhouette for shape-focused reasoning.

The accompanying study evaluates seven vision-language models, four retrieval baselines and 25 human participants.
